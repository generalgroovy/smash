# Play guide

[Back to README](../README.md)

## Start a match

Choose CPU for solo play, Two players for local versus, or Training lab for an inactive dummy. Launch opponents past the stage's blast boundaries to remove stocks. Most damage increases launch strength; reaching a particular percentage is not itself a knockout. A normal match has three stocks and four minutes. Training has unlimited respawns and no time limit.

Duel is the starting point. Flow adds slide and wall routes. Alchemy adds temporary material coatings; stage geometry remains fixed. Match-setting changes restart the round. Visual settings, hitbox display and speed changes do not.

## Controls

Directions aim attacks, specials, throws and dodges. Keyboard bindings use physical positions; `/` also accepts the typed character. On a German keyboard, the punctuation labels may not match the printed keycaps: use the listed numpad alternatives where available. Focus the arena before playing. Losing window focus pauses and clears keyboard/touch input.

| Action | Blue / P1 | Red / P2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / aim | A/D, W/S | Arrows |
| Jump | Space or W | Up arrow |
| Attack | F or J | / |
| Charge smash | Hold T or I, release | Hold ; or Numpad 1, release |
| Shield / dodge / tech | G or L | Right Shift |
| Special | R or K | . or Numpad 2 |
| Grab-throw | E or H | , or Numpad 3 |
| Slide in Flow/Alchemy | C | Right Ctrl |
| Cycle Alchemy element | V | Numpad 0 |

Escape pauses; Backspace restarts; `?` opens controls. **N advances one tick in paused training**, not in a normal match. W doubles as upward aim and jump. Space provides a separate jump input; a gamepad provides independent upward aim.

Standard gamepad: left stick moves/aims, A jumps, X attacks, B specials, Y grabs, RB charges a smash, triggers shield, LB slides, D-pad right cycles elements. The right stick attacks independently of movement: quick smash on the ground, directional aerial in the air. Center it before attacking again. Slots remain stable on disconnect; the match pauses. Physical GameCube adapters require testing/mapping outside the app. Touch layouts expose directional and action buttons; the element selector remains available in Alchemy.

## Five useful techniques

**Short hop.** Tap and release jump during the three-frame jumpsquat. Hold through takeoff for a full hop. A new press in the air uses the air jump.

**Wavedash / waveland.** Jump, then shield plus diagonally down. Landing after the directional air dodge preserves horizontal speed. This is a combination of normal mechanics, not an automatic wavedash button.

**Fast fall and platform drop.** Tap down after the jump apex to descend faster. On a dashed-edge platform, down drops through; the solid main floor cannot be dropped through. Landing lag still has to finish.

**L-cancel and tech.** Tap shield within seven frames before landing an aerial to halve landing lag. Before a hard hitstun landing, a timely shield press enables a tech; add sideways input to roll. Assist L-cancel is optional under Advanced rules.

**Directional influence.** During hit freeze, hold a direction to change the launch angle. The direction is sampled at the end of the freeze. This is not unrestricted steering out of hitstun.

## Attacks with a purpose

Use the in-app **Move workshop** for the complete, engine-derived move list and exact timing; it is the source of truth rather than a duplicated frame table here.

Deliberate repeated attack presses branch Jab into Cross and Heel Turn. Holding attack does not chain. Running into an attack produces Shoulder Drive; a Flow/Alchemy slide can feed Slipstream Sweep. Comet Knee rewards its early contact window. A successful Meteor Heel can rebound while jump is held, once per airtime. Misses, blocks and lost trades do not grant that rebound or restore recovery resources.

Neutral special fires Pulse. Side special is Vector Burst; up special is Skyburn recovery; down special is Flux Field. Air recovery commits you to a helpless ending. Flux reflects during a short active window but is not melee armor. Optional Turbo cancels require a successful hit and a different move; they cannot erase recovery commitments.

Ledges have occupancy and regrab limits. Inward climbs, jump hops away, and down/outward releases. Recover carefully: movement extensions do not refill all your resources.

## Alchemy

Fire ignites oil into a burst. Water meeting fire creates temporary steam lift. Spark charges connected water. Oil preserves horizontal momentum. Effects expire, and both fighters can be harmed. Down special also coats the floor in this mode.

In Alchemy training, choose **Load reaction demo**, or click a platform with a mouse to paint using Blue's selected element. The six material patterns are explained in [Visuals](VISUALS.md).
