STANDARD GAMEPAD

Left stick moves / aims. A jumps, X attacks, B specials, Y grabs. RB smashes, triggers shield, LB slides. Right stick performs directional smashes / aerials independently of movement. D-pad right cycles elements. First pad controls Blue; second controls Red in local versus. Non-standard GameCube adapters need mapping outside the app.

NEW STRINGS & SPECIALS

Jab string: tap attack again late in recovery for Cross, then Heel Turn. Run + attack: Shoulder Drive. Slide + attack: Slipstream Sweep. Side + special: Vector Burst; air use ends helpless. Down + special: Flux Field reflects projectiles during its brief active window, not melee. In Alchemy it also coats the floor. Down air + hold jump on hit: one Meteor Rebound per airtime.

THE GOOD STUFF

Wavedash: jump, then shield + diagonally down. L-cancel: tap shield within 7 frames before landing an aerial. Tech: tap shield before a hard landing during hitstun. DI: hold a direction during hit freeze to change your launch angle. Ledge: inward climbs, jump hops, down drops. Fast fall: tap down after your jump’s apex.
