Info & moves

Win by taking three stocks. Damage makes each hit launch farther. Fast moves open a combo; charged strong attacks finish it. Every attack has a windup and recovery—bait a miss, then punish.

Controls

P1 WASD direction · Space jump · F light · G strong · H special · R dodge.
P2 arrows direction · Enter jump · J light · K strong · L special · right Shift dodge.

P pauses. P2 also supports the physical / . , keys on a US keyboard. Choose a mode or stage for a fresh round. Local 2P needs a keyboard; touch supports P1 against the CPU or practice dummy.

Movement & defense

Release jump early for a short hop. Jump again in the air. Press down while falling to fast-fall; down + jump drops through an upper platform.

Neutral dodge parries briefly. Direction + dodge rolls on the ground or air-dodges once before landing. Up + special recovers once per airtime. Move toward the stage after being launched.

Practice has unlimited stocks. First steps teaches the controls. Combos uses a target that tries to dodge as soon as it can; the counter only joins hits before an escape window. Recovery starts you offstage with one jump: steer inward, jump, then aim up + Special. Landing refills your aerial options. Retry repeats the setup immediately. CPU difficulty changes reaction speed and decisions.
